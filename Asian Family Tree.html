Asian Family Tree

Pick a school year to see its chairs at the top, the mentors below them, and each mentor's mentees beside them. Tap anyone and the tree plays their journey forward year by year.

The tree

Chair (thick trunk = people it carries) Mentor Mentee Ring: started as a mentee New mentor (green line or dot) New chair (gold line or dot) Continued in the same role (dashed line, All years view)
Scroll to zoom and drag to pan. Tap a name to open their card and follow them through the years. Drag to pan, scroll to zoom, tap empty space to clear. Choose All years to see the green, gold and dashed threads between years.

Your log

How the log works. One row per person per year: Year (2026-2027), Person, Uni, Role (Chair, Mentor or Mentee), Mentor (for mentees, their mentor's name or UNI). Any extra columns you add, like School, Major, Hometown or Tags (separate several tags with semicolons), show up on a person's card when you tap them. Photos and tags added on the card itself are saved in this browser only. People are matched across years by UNI, so a name change or two people with the same name never gets mixed up. Rows without a UNI fall back to matching by exact name. In Google Sheets use File, Download, Comma-separated values. The log is remembered in this browser only. This page ships with your current log inside it, so check before sharing the link.
