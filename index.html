Asian Family Tree

Columbia Mentoring Initiative. Pick a school year to see its chairs at the top, the mentors below them, and each mentor's mentees beside them. Tap anyone and the tree plays their journey forward year by year.

The tree

Chair (thick trunk = people it carries) Mentor Mentee Ring: started as a mentee New mentor (green line or dot) New chair (gold line or dot) Continued in the same role (dashed line, All years view)
Scroll to zoom and drag to pan. Tap a name to open their card and follow them through the years. Choose All years to see the green, gold and dashed threads between years.

Data: data.csv in this repository.
